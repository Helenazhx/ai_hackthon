# Valuing the Invisible: Calculate Your Worth

## 🏠 Every Task Counts, Every Hour Matters

For too long, household work has been seen as "just housework." Yet, this vital labor—cooking nutritious meals, maintaining clean spaces, caring for children, managing household supplies—forms the foundation of our society. While this work often goes unpaid, its value is immeasurable.

## 💫 Our Mission

We believe in making the invisible visible. Our Housework Value Calculator helps you:
- Convert your daily tasks into market value
- Understand the real economic impact of your household contributions
- Recognize the professional-level skills you use every day
- Advocate for the value of domestic work in society

## 🎯 How It Works

Simply:
1. Select your country (US/UK/Australia)
2. Check the tasks you perform
3. Input your hours
4. See your work's true market value

## 💝 Why We Created This

Did you know? The UN reports that women worldwide spend 2.5 more times on unpaid domestic work than men. By quantifying this work, we aim to:
- Empower homemakers to recognize their economic contribution
- Support family financial planning discussions
- Highlight the real value of domestic labor in modern society
- Promote gender equality in household work recognition
